# Victoria Council Parking Coverage (2026-04-21)

## Scope

This audit compares the latest Victoria parking research export against the 79 official Victorian councils. It measures whether the workspace currently contains at least one official council-owned public parking dataset for each council.

- Source export: `victoria-public-parking-zones-by-suburb-2026-04-17.csv`
- Official Victorian councils tracked: 79
- Councils with official parking data in workspace: 4
- Councils without official parking data in workspace: 75

## Covered councils

- Ballarat City Council: Car Parking Zones | Parking Meters
- Casey City Council: City of Casey Parking Restriction Zones
- Greater Geelong City Council: Parking Lots
- Melbourne City Council: On-street Parking Bay Sensors | On-street Parking Bays | Parking zones linked to street segments
